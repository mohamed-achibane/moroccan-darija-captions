# Starting caption style

This is the starting preset from the skill author’s approved edit. Treat it as a selectable default; honor another user’s stated design.

- 1080×1920 vertical. Preserve source image, audio, framing, and opening title.
- Use word-level timing across the whole clip, with natural phrase groups in the transcript.
- Display two consecutive words; highlight only the active spoken word in `#9DDBF5`; keep the other visible word `#FFF9F0`.
- Keep Arabic in logical reading order with `dir="rtl"` and `lang="ar"`. Put Latin words on a dedicated row, isolate them with `dir="ltr"`, and uppercase only at display time.
- Arabic font: Myriad Arabic Bold, weight 700, 140px, 2px current-color stroke. If unavailable, tell the user before substituting.
- Latin font: Arial Bold, fixed 100px.
- Center the caption rows in the lower safe area, approximately 300px above the bottom, at one consistent level from the opening through the end. Keep them below the title and clear of the speaker’s face.
- Use a subtle shadow and restrained fade/lift. Do not make words jump or change order.
- No background plate, frame, icons, or decorative graphics. Remove punctuation from displayed captions only when requested; keep transcript text unchanged.
