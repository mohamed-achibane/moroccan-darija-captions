# Credits

Moroccan Darija Captions was created by **Mohamed Achibane**. The official profile links are listed in the [README](README.md).

The workflow design was informed by the public [Darija word captions project](https://github.com/benmouad-ai/darija-word-captions), especially its separation of transcription from word alignment and its attention to Moroccan Darija mixed with French and English.

This repository has its own platform adapters, project-preservation rules, caption preset, and transcript validator. It does not include that project’s alignment script.
